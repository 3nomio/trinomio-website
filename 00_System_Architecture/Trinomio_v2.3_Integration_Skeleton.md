- The macro chain **feeds into** a single container; all operations **compile inside**
  it. That container is the **Strategy Cube**: `ΔTech × ΔReg × NodalValue`, with
  **NodalValue ≡ P(n,t)**.
- Everything built this session is a statement about position or motion in the cube:
  `m`, `MEV`, `EFI` are cube outputs; **D=0 is the cube's vertex**; **23.414** is a
  step along ΔReg; the **Four Arenas** are regions; the **fade loop** is a trajectory.
- Presentation ceiling = the cube. No constructs live outside it; `ΔCapital` +
  commercial contract enter only as the **deployment gate** on a cube point.
